Enrichment Forge

Find a series or standalone book already in your Audiobookshelf library. Every book is looked up in Audible, Goodreads, AudioSilo and Open Library, and the series in progressionfantasy.co.uk and the HaremLit wiki. The genres they agree on are compiled for you to review, then applied to every included book at once.

Heads up: Apply pushes directly to Audiobookshelf via its API and never edits the audio files' own tags. If a book's folder still has an old metadata.json from earlier LibraForge versions, it would undo this change on the next scan, so it is first compared with Audiobookshelf (the newer side wins) and then removed. Only if Audiobookshelf isn't configured does this write to metadata.json instead; in that case, if the library has Audiobookshelf's "Store metadata with item" setting enabled, ABS regenerates metadata.json itself on its next scan and will overwrite what you applied here.
